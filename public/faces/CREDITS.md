# Faces

All four portraits are AI-generated with Canva on 2026-11-01 and show nobody real.
They stay labelled as a sample face or sample creator wherever they appear in the app.

- `sample.jpg`: the try-on sample face.
- `ines.jpg`, `amara.jpg`, `lucie.jpg`: sample creators.

Each prompt asked for a photorealistic portrait of a fictional adult woman on a pure black
background, looking straight into the camera, with the makeup of that creator's style.
Salma, Chloé and Noor have no portrait yet.
